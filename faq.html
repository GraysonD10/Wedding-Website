Before The Day

Planning Notes

When should I RSVP by?

Please RSVP by August 1st, 2026 so we can finalize our guest count.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Please refer to the name listed on your invitation or RSVP form. If a guest is included, they will appear there.

Are children invited?

We love your little ones, but our wedding celebration will be adults-only unless otherwise noted on your invitation.

Where are you registered?

You can find our registry on the Registry page. Your presence is truly the greatest gift.

Wedding Day

Schedule & Place

Where is the wedding?

The wedding will be at 215 Carrolls Way, Fayetteville, Georgia.

What time should I arrive?

Guests should arrive around 4:30 PM so everyone has time to park and find a seat before the ceremony begins at 5:00 PM.

Will the ceremony be outdoors?

Yes, the ceremony is planned for the open pasture, weather permitting.

Will dinner be served?

Yes, supper and dancing will follow the ceremony and cocktail hour.

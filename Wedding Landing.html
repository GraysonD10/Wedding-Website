01 Chapter One

Our Story

We knew each other for a few years through mutual friends, crossing paths here and there but never getting the chance to know each other. From the beginning, there was always something there, it just took the right moment for things to fall into place.

That moment came after a concert and, not long after, a spontaneous trip to Florida where we spent days together and talked for hours on a balcony overlooking the ocean. Somewhere in those conversations, everything started to feel different.

When we got back, we did not waste any time. One date turned into many, and just a few weeks later, what started as something simple turned into something we knew was worth holding onto. Since then, we have built a relationship full of love, laughter, trust, and adventure, and we cannot wait to celebrate this next chapter with you.

— Emme & Grayson
